# Overlap Policy

Retrieval belongs to `rumi_research_pack`; private connector access belongs to `rumi_connector_gateway_pack`; data transforms belong to `rumi_data_analysis_pack`; document parsing/rendering belongs to `rumi_document_intelligence_pack`; workspace export belongs to `rumi_workspace_pack`; model scoring belongs to `rumi_model_evals_pack`. This pack owns only provenance contracts, claim/evidence graphs, citation ledgers, contradiction review contracts, reviewer queues, and export manifest shape.
