# Operations

Start every media workflow by recording the source and allowed use. For screenshots, redact visible secrets and account identifiers before sharing outside the local workspace. For generated assets, keep the prompt, references, dimensions, and rejection notes in the asset ledger.

A media task is complete only when the asset ledger and review checklist support the final handoff. Visual appeal alone is not enough; Rumi should also check readability, accessibility, factual grounding, and whether the artifact fits the target surface.
