# Rumi Research Pack

`rumi_research_pack` is an optional, declarative, local-first ecosystem pack for deep research and evidence handling. It gives Rumi a native vocabulary for research plans, evidence cards, source quality, citation workflows, claim tracing, synthesis reports, and research handoffs.

## What It Provides

- Research capability and workflow catalogs.
- Evidence card and citation metadata schemas.
- Profiles for deep research, evidence review, and local-only synthesis.
- Presets inspired by deep research reports, Genspark-style comparison reports, and citation-heavy Gemini/Claude workflows.
- Prompt templates for planning, evidence extraction, citation discipline, contradiction handling, and final synthesis.
- Examples for market scans, literature-style evidence reviews, and decision briefs.

## What It Does Not Provide

- No executable tools, handlers, routes, scrapers, browsers, crawlers, or provider clients.
- No embedded credentials, tokens, private endpoints, or external API keys.
- No automatic network access. Network posture is `none_by_default`.
- No replacement for `defaultspack`; this pack only adds research-facing declarations.
