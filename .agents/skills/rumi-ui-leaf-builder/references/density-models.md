# Density Models

Comfortable:
- Primary reading and composition tasks.
- Wider padding and clear group spacing.
- Secondary actions may be progressively disclosed.

Compact:
- Repeated operational lists and moderate dashboards.
- Group spacing can be tighter, but labels and primary controls must remain readable.

DataDense:
- Tables, calendars, and scan-heavy surfaces.
- Density comes from structure, alignment, and progressive disclosure, not from shrinking text below role size.
